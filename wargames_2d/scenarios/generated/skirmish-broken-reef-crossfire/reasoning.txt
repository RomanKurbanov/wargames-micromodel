This scenario is a **Skirmish**: both factions field only a handful of ships, their weapons are decisively short-ranged, and the map is packed with obstacles that constantly break sightlines. I used an **8-ship agile raider group** against a **6-ship heavier breacher group** so the engagement stays tactical rather than purely symmetrical. The raiders accelerate and turn faster, letting them exploit gaps and collapse isolated targets, while the breachers are individually tougher and hit harder inside brawling range.

The dense obstacle field is the real teacher here. It creates repeated situations where ships must path around cover, avoid collisions, briefly lose targets, then re-engage at awkward angles. That makes the battle useful for training close-quarters behaviors: peeking around terrain, deciding when to chase versus brake, focus-firing in clutter, and surviving short-range ambushes without devolving into a simple open-field joust.
