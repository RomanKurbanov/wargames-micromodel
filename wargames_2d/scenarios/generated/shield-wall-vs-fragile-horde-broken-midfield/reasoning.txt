This scenario is a **Shield wall vs fragile horde** matchup. The shield-wall faction fields only 12 slow, disciplined ships with large shields, modest regeneration, narrow firing arcs, and medium range. Opposing them is a 32-ship horde built around speed, wide engagement angles, and extremely high sustained close-range fire, but with almost no staying power. The stat line is tuned so the wall wins if it keeps formation and trades frontally, while the horde wins if it collapses a flank, surrounds isolated targets, or uses cover to enter its shorter effective range.

The central obstacle belt is intentional: it prevents the shield wall from enjoying a perfectly clean firing line and creates pockets where the horde can stage short rushes. This makes the battle educational for AI training because it stresses target focus, formation coherence, shield pressure timing, pathing around terrain, and the difference between durable area control and fragile burst swarming. It should produce varied engagements: some runs become orderly shield advances, while others turn into chaotic multi-angle breaches once the horde finds a gap.
