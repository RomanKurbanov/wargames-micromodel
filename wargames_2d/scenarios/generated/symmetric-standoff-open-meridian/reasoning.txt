This scenario is a **symmetric standoff**: two identical factions start on opposite sides of the map with mirrored positions, mirrored objectives, and exactly the same ship and weapon stats. I chose medium-long weapon range, moderate mobility, and narrow firing arcs so the fleets cannot simply rush or instantly erase each other. Instead, they tend to form lines, probe for angle advantages, and punish units that drift out of formation.

The open field makes the symmetry clean and readable for training: with no obstacles deciding the battle, outcomes depend on spacing, target selection, braking discipline, and when ships commit across the centerline. Moderate shields and limited shield regeneration let damaged ships survive long enough to disengage and re-enter, which is useful for teaching the AI how to maintain pressure without overextending in an otherwise even matchup.
