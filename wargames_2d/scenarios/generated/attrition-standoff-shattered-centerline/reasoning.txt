This scenario is an **attrition standoff**: two slow, durable battle lines with long reload cycles grind for control of the center instead of exploding into a fast alpha strike. I kept both factions at equal ship counts so the lesson is about timing, formation discipline, and target selection rather than raw numerical asymmetry. The stats are deliberately heavy and conservative: modest speed, high effective HP, narrow-to-moderate firing arcs, and reloads in the 11–12 second range create clear engagement windows where ships must survive incoming salvos, reposition, and then recommit.

The tactical contrast is subtle but important. Faction 0 has a slightly higher sustained cycle DPS, a broader firing angle, and more shielded durability, making it better in close coordinated volleys and line maintenance. Faction 1 trades some sustained output for longer range, heavier per-shot damage, and more hull, rewarding careful spacing and early volley placement. Sparse central obstacles break perfect firing lanes without turning the battle into a maze, which makes this useful for training AI behaviors such as holding contact at the edge of range, pushing during enemy reload downtime, avoiding overextension, and preserving formation under slow-grind pressure.
