This scenario is a classic **swarm vs sniper** matchup: a large raiding pack of fast, short-range ships must cross open space and fragmented cover to overwhelm a compact group of slow, durable long-range gunships. I chose a high speed and turn rate for the swarm so they can exploit gaps, dodge during reload windows, and collapse onto isolated snipers, while the sniper faction gets heavy effective durability, narrow firing arcs, and long reach so positioning and target discipline matter.

The battle is designed to teach several useful behaviors at once. The swarm side must learn approach timing, focus fire at close range, and how to use obstacles to break sightlines instead of charging straight into volleys. The sniper side must learn spacing, threat prioritization, and how to preserve a firing line without overextending. Because the numbers are asymmetrical but still power-balanced, the engagement should produce a real midbattle transition: early sniper advantage at range, then a dangerous close-range scramble if the swarm reaches the line.
