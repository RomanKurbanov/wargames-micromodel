This scenario is a classic **swarm vs sniper** matchup: a large pack of fast, lightly protected attackers must cross broken midfield cover to collapse on a much smaller line of durable long-range marksmen. The swarm ships are deliberately built to accelerate hard, turn quickly, and dump short-range fire in bursts once they get inside 220 pixels. The sniper faction is slow and narrow-angled, with strong per-shot damage and long 620-pixel reach, so they reward formation discipline and target prioritization but can be overwhelmed if they let the swarm penetrate their firing envelope.

The obstacle field is tuned to create partial approach lanes rather than total clutter. That makes the battle educational for micro-AI because both sides must constantly trade off pathing, spacing, and threat response: swarm units need to weave through cover, avoid bunching, and commit to local flanks, while sniper ships must maintain standoff distance, avoid getting surrounded, and keep firing arcs clear. The stat balance keeps both sides survivable through initial contact and prevents a trivial wipe, producing a battle where positioning and timing matter more than raw first-volley advantage.
