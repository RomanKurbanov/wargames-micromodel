This scenario is a classic **swarm vs sniper** matchup: a large pack of fast, lightly protected attack craft must cross broken terrain and overwhelm a much smaller group of slow, durable long-range gunships. The swarm ships are tuned for acceleration, turning, and sustained close-range pressure, but their short weapon range forces them to commit into danger. The sniper ships have narrow firing arcs, high per-shot damage, and long sightlines, so they reward good positioning and punish frontal approaches, but their low speed and modest sustained DPS mean they can be surrounded if they lose spacing.

The balance is set so neither side should evaporate on first contact. Swarm ships are fragile but still survive a single sniper hit, while the sniper faction has clearly stronger individual combat power without exceeding the total-power cap. Moderate obstacles create approach lanes and partial cover, teaching the AI to flank, spread, kite, and decide when to push through fire versus when to regroup. This makes the battle useful for training target selection, approach timing, range control, and formation breakup under asymmetric pressure.
